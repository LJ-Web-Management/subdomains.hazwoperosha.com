Reading the packing group letter

FAQ

Which regulation does the course cover?

The packaging requirements in 49 CFR Part 173 of the DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations. DOT hazmat training requirements are in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H.

What's the difference between a package and packaging?

Packaging is the receptacle and its components. A package is the packaging plus its contents, as prepared for shipment. The course explains how the HMR uses each term.

How often must I retrain?

Hazmat employees must receive recurrent training at least once every three years, and training must be updated when DOT issues new or revised regulations that affect their job. The certificate is valid for three years.

Are there prerequisites?

No. It builds on the 4-hour DOT Hazmat Transportation General Awareness course, but there are no prerequisites. The course is available in English and Spanish.
