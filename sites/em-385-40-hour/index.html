Sec. 1 Who needs EM 385-1-1 training

EM 385-1-1 compliance is mandatory on federal and military construction projects that follow USACE safety standards. This course was built for:

  • Site Safety and Health Officers (SSHOs), who run site-specific safety programs. The course addresses the formal training part of SSHO qualification.
  • Contractors and subcontractors in construction, demolition, environmental remediation and service contracts for military or federal agencies.
  • Construction managers, foremen and supervisors who oversee workers and project safety compliance.
  • Government and military personnel handling oversight, safety enforcement or contract administration on USACE job sites.
  • QC managers, project engineers and safety consultants who apply EM 385-1-1 in field operations.

Sec. 2 What you will be able to do

  1. Interpret and apply EM 385-1-1 to daily jobsite operations.
  2. Write required documentation, including Activity Hazard Analyses (AHAs) and Accident Prevention Plans (APPs).
  3. Support and enforce a compliant safety culture.
  4. Meet the safety training requirements for workers, competent persons, supervisors and SSHOs on federal worksites.

Sec. 5 Questions and answers

How long is the EM 385-1-1 40-hour course?

The course enforces minimum seat time per lesson, so completion takes approximately 40 hours. You have 3 months to finish and 1 year of access to the materials.

Is this EM 385-1-1 course government approved?

No EM 385-1-1 course is government approved. The U.S. Federal Government and its agencies do not officially approve or endorse any EM 385-1-1 training provider. The requirement is that people on federal or military construction sites receive training that meets the EM 385-1-1 safety standards. On completion you receive a Certificate of Completion issued by HAZWOPER OSHA Training LLC.

How is EM 385-1-1 40-hour training different from OSHA Outreach construction training?

EM 385-1-1 40-hour training includes OSHA standards but is more stringent, adding requirements from NFPA, EPA, DOT, ANSI and UFGS. It also covers topics OSHA Outreach does not, such as accident prevention plans, program management and military job site inspections.

What score do I need to pass?

You need 70% or higher on the final exam. You have 3 attempts; after a failed attempt you review the related modules before trying again.

Are there any prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites for the EM 385-1-1 40-Hour Training course.

When do I get my certificate and how long is it valid?

You can download, save or print your PDF certificate immediately after passing the final exam. It is valid for one year; 8-hour EM 385-1-1 refresher courses are available for workers and for SSHOs.
