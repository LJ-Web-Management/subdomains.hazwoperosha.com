FAQ

Which regulation does the course cover?

The shipping paper requirements in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart C of the DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations. DOT hazmat training requirements are in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H.

Why do shipping papers matter?

Shipping papers identify the hazardous materials being transported and give first responders essential emergency response information. Failing to properly describe hazmat on shipping papers is one of the most frequently cited HMR violations.

Does it cover the hazardous waste manifest?

Yes. The course explains the purpose of the hazardous waste manifest and the information it must include, along with recordkeeping requirements.

How often must I retrain?

Hazmat employees must receive recurrent training at least once every three years. The certificate is valid for three years.
