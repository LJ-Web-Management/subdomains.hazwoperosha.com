This year's checklist

Seven modules, taken in order, then a final exam:

  1. Regulatory overview

    Title 8 §5192 and HAZWOPER requirements, hazard communication and GHS, safety data sheets, medical surveillance and recordkeeping.

  2. Toxicology

    Common toxic substances, exposure routes and target organ effects.

  3. Hazard classification

    Job hazard analysis, physical and chemical properties, fire and oxygen deficiency, bloodborne pathogens and radiological hazards.

  4. Personal protective equipment

    Respiratory protection, respirator types, chemical protective clothing, and head-to-toe PPE.

  5. HAZWOPER site operations

    Site safety and health plans, site control and work zones, air monitoring, direct-reading instruments and confined space entry.

  6. Material sampling & analysis

    Field and environmental sampling plans and drum handling.

  7. Emergency response procedures

    Decontamination stations and lines, facility design, methods and site emergencies.

Notes & questions

Who needs the Cal/OSHA 8-hour HAZWOPER refresher?

Workers in uncontrolled hazardous waste or emergency response operations who have completed initial HAZWOPER training, including equipment operators, general laborers, other site workers, and on-site managers and supervisors. They must complete 8 hours of refresher training every year.

Which regulations does the refresher meet?

Cal/OSHA Title 8, Section 5192, paragraphs (a) to (q), and Federal OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120(e) and 29 CFR 1926.65(e).

Do I have to finish the course in one sitting?

No. You can start and stop and your progress is saved. However, once you begin a section, finish it in the same session, or that section restarts from the beginning next time you log in.

How long is the certificate valid?

One year. Download your PDF certificate immediately after passing the final exam, and retake the refresher every 12 months.

Can I get a wallet ID card?

Yes, as an add-on purchase. Upload a passport-style photo to your account and the plastic card is mailed within 2–3 weeks.
