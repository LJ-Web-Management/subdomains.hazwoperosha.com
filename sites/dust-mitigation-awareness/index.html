FAQ

Which OSHA standards does the course cover?

Key requirements of the OSHA construction standards (29 CFR 1926), general industry standards (29 CFR 1910) and the HAZWOPER standard, including permissible exposure limits and monitoring obligations for dust.

Who should take it?

Anyone whose work creates or disturbs dust: construction laborers, demolition, masonry and paving crews, heavy equipment operators, miners and drillers, renovation workers, remediation teams, quarry and manufacturing workers, and the supervisors who oversee them.

How long is the certificate valid?

Three years. Retake the course within 36 months to stay current.

Are there prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites. The course is available in English.
