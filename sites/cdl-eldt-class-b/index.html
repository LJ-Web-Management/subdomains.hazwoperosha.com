FAQ

What vehicles need a Class B CDL?

Single vehicles with a gross vehicle weight rating (GVWR) of 26,001 pounds or more, such as large straight trucks, delivery trucks, dump trucks, and many school, transit and shuttle buses, including such a vehicle towing a trailer of 10,000 pounds GVWR or less.

Is this ELDT Class B course accepted in every state?

It is accepted in every state except Washington, which requires classroom instruction by a registered Washington training provider for drivers who hold or are obtaining a Washington CDL.

Does the course include behind-the-wheel training?

No. This is the theory portion. If your state requires behind-the-wheel training, complete it separately with an approved provider found through your state DMV or DOT or the FMCSA Training Provider Registry.

How does the FMCSA know I completed the training?

When you pass the final exam with 80% or higher, your completion is reported to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry.

What are the prerequisites?

Typically: being at least 18 (21 to drive across state lines), holding a valid non-commercial driver's license in the state where you train, passing a DOT physical, and meeting any other state or provider requirements.
