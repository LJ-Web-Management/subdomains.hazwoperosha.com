FAQ

Which regulations does it cover?

The modal-specific requirements in 49 CFR Part 174 (Carriage by Rail) of the DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations. DOT hazmat training requirements are in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H.

How often must I retrain?

Hazmat employees must receive recurrent training at least once every three years. The certificate is valid for three years.

Are there prerequisites?

No. It builds on the 4-hour DOT Hazmat Transportation General Awareness course, but there are no prerequisites. The course is available in English and Spanish.

Does it cover key trains?

Yes. The course summarizes the Association of American Railroads recommendations for the safe operation of key trains, along with switching and positioning restrictions for rail cars carrying hazmat.
