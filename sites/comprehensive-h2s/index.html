Know the numbers

Key U.S. exposure limits for hydrogen sulfide. Exposure limits and gas monitoring are covered in Modules 1 and 2. Never rely on smell: H₂S can deaden your sense of smell.

  1. 10 ppmOSHA construction PEL, 8-hour time-weighted average (29 CFR 1926)
  2. 20 ppmOSHA general industry ceiling limit (29 CFR 1910)
  3. 50 ppmOSHA general industry peak, up to 10 minutes once per 8-hour shift if no other measurable exposure occurs
  4. 100 ppmNIOSH Immediately Dangerous to Life or Health (IDLH)

FAQ

Which standards does the course follow?

It follows ANSI/ASSP Z390.1-2024, Accepted Practices for H₂S Training Programs, and API RP 49 and 55, and covers OSHA 29 CFR 1910 and 1926 and BSEE 30 CFR 250.490.

Is refresher training required?

Yes. ANSI Z390.1 calls for annual H₂S retraining. Your certificate is valid for one year; retake the course within 12 months to stay current.

Are there prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites, and the course is available in English and Spanish.

Is there a shorter awareness-level option?

Yes. See the OSHA Hydrogen Sulfide (H₂S) Awareness course for awareness-level training.
