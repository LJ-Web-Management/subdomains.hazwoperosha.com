FAQ

Who is this refresher for?

Site Safety and Health Officers who have completed their initial EM 385-1-1 training and need the 8 hours of annual refresher training required on USACE projects.

Are there prerequisites?

There are no formal prerequisites, but the course is designed for SSHOs who have already completed initial EM 385-1-1 training.

Is this course government-approved?

No. The federal government and its agencies do not approve or endorse any EM 385-1-1 training course. You receive a certificate of completion from HAZWOPER OSHA Training.

When does the certificate expire?

After one year. Take the course again before your certificate expires to stay current. It is available in English and Spanish.
