FAQ

What happens if a supplier never sends the SDS in California?

The employer must send a written inquiry to the manufacturer or importer within 7 working days. If there is no response within 25 working days, the employer must send a copy of the inquiry to the Director of Industrial Relations.

How does Proposition 65 relate to HazCom training?

California's HazCom regulation includes Proposition 65 provisions. For chemicals covered by both, employers subject to Proposition 65 can meet its employee warning requirements by following Section 5194. Employers with fewer than 10 employees are not subject to Proposition 65.

How is Cal/OSHA HazCom different from the federal standard?

Title 8 Section 5194 adds California-only requirements, including the Director's List of Hazardous Substances, a plain-language health risk description on SDSs, a 30-day rule for sharing revised SDS information, and a written missing-SDS inquiry procedure. It also applies to state, county and city employers.

When is HazCom training required?

At an employee's initial assignment and whenever a new chemical hazard is introduced into the work area. Annual refresher training is recommended as best practice.
