FAQ

Is ELDT required before the School Bus (S) knowledge test?

Yes. Since February 2022, FMCSA has required entry-level drivers to complete approved training before taking the knowledge test for the S endorsement.

What do I need before enrolling?

A valid commercial learner's permit (CLP) or CDL of the appropriate class, Class B or Class C, depending on the type of school bus you will operate.

Is this the only requirement for the S endorsement?

No. You also need a medical exam from an examiner on FMCSA's National Registry of Certified Medical Examiners, a background check and fingerprinting, the state school bus knowledge test, and skills tests taken in the type of school bus you intend to drive. Check with your state licensing agency for its exact requirements.

Is behind-the-wheel training included?

No. This course covers the ELDT theory requirement. If your state requires behind-the-wheel training, complete it separately with a provider listed on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry.

Is this course accepted in every state?

It is accepted in every state except Washington, which requires classroom instruction by a registered Washington training provider for drivers who hold or are obtaining a Washington CDL.
