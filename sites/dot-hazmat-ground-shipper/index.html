FAQ

Does this course replace my employer's hazmat training?

No. Completing this course does not by itself replace an employer's responsibility to provide function-specific training that matches each employee's actual hazmat duties. Hazmat employees must be trained and tested by their hazmat employer as required.

Which regulations does it cover?

The DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations in 49 CFR Parts 171-180, including the hazmat employee training requirements in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H, and Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations that apply to highway shipments.

How often must I retrain?

DOT requires hazmat employees to receive recurrent training at least once every three years. The certificate is valid for three years.

Are there prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites, and the course is available in English and Spanish.
