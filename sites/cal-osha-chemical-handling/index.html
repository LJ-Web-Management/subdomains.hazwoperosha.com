FAQ

What is passivation in metal finishing?

Passivation is a chemical method that improves a metal's corrosion resistance by forming a protective film on its surface. The chemicals used are health and safety hazards if not handled properly.

Why are solvents and oxidizers hazardous?

Solvents can be flammable, explosive and harmful to health. Oxidizers intensify combustion and react dangerously with incompatible materials. Safe storage, handling and exposure controls reduce these hazards.

Which Cal/OSHA standards does the course cover?

California Code of Regulations Title 8, Sections 3203 (Injury and Illness Prevention Program), 5194 (Hazard Communication), 5141, 5154 and 5162 through 5165.

How often is chemical handling training required?

Under Title 8 Section 5194, hazard communication training must be given at initial assignment and whenever a new chemical hazard is introduced. Annual refresher training is recommended as a best practice, or sooner when hazards or procedures change. The certificate is valid for one year.
