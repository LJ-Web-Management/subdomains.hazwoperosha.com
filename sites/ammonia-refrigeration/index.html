Common questions

Which OSHA standard does the ammonia refrigeration course cover?

The course meets the requirements of OSHA General Industry Standard 29 CFR 1910.111, Storage and Handling of Anhydrous Ammonia.

Who should take ammonia refrigeration safety awareness training?

Facility managers, maintenance technicians, refrigeration operators, engineers, EHS professionals and safety personnel who work with or around industrial ammonia refrigeration, for example in cold storage, food processing, manufacturing and distribution facilities.

Are there any prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites for the Ammonia Refrigeration Basics and Safety Awareness Training course.

How long is the certificate valid?

The certificate is valid for three years. Employers are recommended to provide refresher training at least once every three years, so retake the course within 36 months to stay current.

When do I receive my certificate?

Immediately after passing the final exam you can download, save or print your certificate as a PDF.
