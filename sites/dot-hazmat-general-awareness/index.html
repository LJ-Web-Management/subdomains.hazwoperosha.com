FAQ

Who needs DOT hazmat general awareness training?

Hazmat employers and employees who prepare hazardous materials for shipment or engage in their commercial transportation, including people who package, mark, label, load, unload or handle hazmat packages, and drivers responsible for hazmat in transit.

Do I need this training to sign a hazardous waste manifest?

Yes. Anyone who offers a hazardous material, including hazardous waste, for transportation signs a certification that it is properly described, classified, packaged, marked and labeled, so they must be trained.

How often must I retrain?

Hazmat employees must receive recurrent training at least once every three years. This course can be taken for both initial and refresher training, and the certificate is valid for three years.

Is there a more detailed course?

Yes. The 10-Hour DOT Hazmat Transportation Advanced General Awareness course adds function-specific instruction and modal requirements for highway, rail, air and vessel.
