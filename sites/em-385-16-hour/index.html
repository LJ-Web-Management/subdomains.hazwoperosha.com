More than OSHA Outreach

EM 385-1-1 builds on OSHA standards but is more stringent, drawing on NFPA, EPA, DOT, ANSI and UFGS requirements. It also covers topics OSHA Outreach doesn't, such as accident prevention plans, program management and military jobsite inspections.

FAQ

Who is this course for?

Non-supervisory workers and contractors on construction or environmental projects directed by the U.S. Department of Defense and administered by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, or other federally funded projects that follow EM 385-1-1.

Is this course government-approved?

No. The federal government and its agencies do not approve or endorse any EM 385-1-1 training course, so no provider can offer a government-approved certificate. You receive a certificate of completion from HAZWOPER OSHA Training.

How is it different from OSHA Outreach training?

EM 385-1-1 includes OSHA standards but is more stringent, drawing on NFPA, EPA, DOT, ANSI and UFGS requirements, and covers topics like accident prevention plans, program management and military jobsite inspections.

Is refresher training required?

Yes. The certificate is valid for 12 months; complete the 8-Hour EM 385-1-1 Refresher Training for Workers annually.
