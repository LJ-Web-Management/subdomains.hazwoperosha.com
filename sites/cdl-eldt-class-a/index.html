Who needs Class A

Anyone who will operate combination vehicles with a GCWR of 26,001 lb or more when towing a trailer over 10,000 lb:

  • Tractor-trailer, semi-truck and big-rig operators
  • Long-haul and regional trucking drivers
  • Freight, logistics, construction and agriculture haulers
  • Drivers of flatbeds, tankers and livestock trailers
  • New drivers who must meet FMCSA ELDT requirements

FAQ

Who needs a Class A CDL?

Drivers who will operate combination vehicles with a gross combination weight rating (GCWR) of 26,001 pounds or more when towing a trailer over 10,000 pounds, such as tractor-trailers, semi-trucks, flatbeds, tankers and livestock trailers.

Is this ELDT Class A course accepted in every state?

It is accepted in every state except Washington, which requires classroom instruction by a registered Washington training provider for drivers who hold or are obtaining a Washington CDL.

Does this course include behind-the-wheel training?

No. This is the theory portion. If your state requires behind-the-wheel training, complete it separately with an approved provider found through your state DMV or DOT or the FMCSA Training Provider Registry.

What score do I need to pass?

You must pass the final exam with a score of 80% or higher. Once you pass, your completion is reported to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry.

What are the prerequisites?

Typically: being at least 18 (21 to drive across state lines), holding a valid non-commercial driver's license in the state where you train, and meeting any additional state or provider requirements.
