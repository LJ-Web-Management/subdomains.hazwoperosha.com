FAQ

What is personal protective equipment (PPE)?

PPE is equipment worn to minimize exposure to hazards that can cause serious workplace injuries and illnesses, such as gloves, safety glasses, face shields, hard hats, hearing protection, respirators, safety shoes, chemical protective clothing and electrical protective equipment.

Which Cal/OSHA standards does the PPE course cover?

Title 8 CCR Sections 3380–3385 (General Industry Safety Orders) and Section 1514 (Construction Safety Orders), plus related standards including Section 5144 (Respiratory Protection) and Section 5097 (Hearing Conservation Program).

How often is PPE training required?

Cal/OSHA does not set a fixed refresher period. Under Title 8 Section 3380, each employee who must use PPE has to be trained before doing work that requires it, and retrained when the workplace or type of PPE changes.

Where does PPE fit in the hierarchy of controls?

PPE is the final level of protection, used only when elimination, engineering controls and administrative controls are not enough on their own.
