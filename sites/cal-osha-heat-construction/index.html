Know the warning signs as they escalate

  1. Heat cramps

    Early warning: muscle cramps during heavy work in heat.

  2. Heat exhaustion

    The body is struggling to cool itself. Stop, cool down and get help.

  3. Heat stroke

    A medical emergency. Follow your site's emergency response procedures.

Recognizing the signs and symptoms in yourself and coworkers is a core course objective.

Who should take it

Indoor workers

Factory operators in hot areas, warehouse associates, maintenance techs near heat sources, kitchen staff.

Supervisors & managers

Site, production line and warehouse supervisors, and facility managers enforcing heat rules.

Safety professionals

Safety officers writing Heat Illness Prevention Plans, risk managers, occupational health trainers.

People new to hot environments, who are usually the most at risk because they are not yet acclimatized.

Outdoor event volunteers and community service staff working in high temperatures.

FAQ

Which regulations does this heat illness course cover?

Cal/OSHA Title 8, Section 3395 (outdoor heat illness prevention) and Section 3396 (indoor heat illness prevention), and the federal OSH Act General Duty Clause, Section 5(a)(1).

Is a refresher required for heat illness prevention training?

Yes. Refresher training is required every 12 months, and the course certificate is valid for one year.

How long does the course take?

About 1 hour: 4 lessons followed by a final exam.

Are there any prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites for the Cal/OSHA Heat Illness Prevention Training for Construction course.
