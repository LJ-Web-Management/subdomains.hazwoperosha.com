Before you enroll

  • Theory only. Behind-the-wheel training is not included. If your state requires it, complete it with an approved provider (find one through your state DMV or DOT, or the FMCSA Training Provider Registry).
  • Washington drivers: Washington requires classroom instruction by a registered Washington training provider for CDL certifications and endorsements. The course is accepted in every other state.
  • Prerequisites typically include being 18 or older (21 for interstate driving), a valid Class B license, and a DOT physical and drug screen.

FAQ

How does the FMCSA know I completed the training?

Once you complete and pass the course, your completion is reported to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry.

Is this ELDT course accepted in every state?

It is accepted in every state except Washington. Washington requires classroom instruction by a registered Washington training provider for all CDL certifications and endorsements; this applies only to drivers who hold or are obtaining a Washington CDL.

Does this course include behind-the-wheel training?

No. This is the theory portion. Some states also require behind-the-wheel training, which must be completed separately with an approved provider, found through your state DMV or DOT or the FMCSA Training Provider Registry.

What score do I need to pass?

You must pass the final exam with a score of 80% or higher.

What are the prerequisites?

Typically: being at least 18 (21 to drive across state lines), holding a valid Class B license in the state where you train, passing a DOT physical and drug screen, and meeting any additional state or provider requirements.
