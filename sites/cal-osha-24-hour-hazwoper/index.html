Questions

Which regulations does the Cal/OSHA 24-hour HAZWOPER course cover?

Cal/OSHA HAZWOPER regulations in Title 8, Section 5192, paragraphs (a) to (q), and Federal OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120(e) and 29 CFR 1926.65(e).

Is a refresher required after Cal/OSHA 24-hour HAZWOPER training?

Yes. An annual 8-hour HAZWOPER refresher is required under Cal/OSHA Title 8, Section 5192 to stay compliant after completing 24-hour training. The certificate is valid for 12 months.

Who needs Cal/OSHA 24-hour HAZWOPER training?

California workers in hazardous waste cleanup at contaminated sites, TSD facility employees, construction crews doing excavation or demolition near hazardous substances, environmental technicians, utility workers exposed to contaminated soil or groundwater, and the supervisors who oversee them.

Are there any prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites for the Cal/OSHA 24-Hour HAZWOPER Training course.
