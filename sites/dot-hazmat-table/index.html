FAQ

Why is the Hazardous Materials Table so important?

Compliance with the DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations centers on the Hazardous Materials Table. It indicates the requirements for preparing hazmat for transportation, including identification, packaging, labeling and mode-specific requirements.

Which regulation does the course cover?

The Hazardous Materials Table in 49 CFR 172.101 (Part 172 Subpart B) and its Appendices A and B. DOT hazmat training requirements are in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H.

How often must I retrain?

Hazmat employees must receive recurrent training at least once every three years, and training must be updated when DOT issues new or revised regulations that affect their job. The certificate is valid for three years.

Are there prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites, and the course is available in English and Spanish.
