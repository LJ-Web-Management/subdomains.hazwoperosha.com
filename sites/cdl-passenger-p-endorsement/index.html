Your path to the P endorsement

  1. Hold a valid CDL and a medical certificate from an examiner on FMCSA's National Registry.
  2. Complete this ELDT theory course. We report your completion to the Training Provider Registry.
  3. Pass the state passenger knowledge test at your motor vehicle office.
  4. Pass the skills test: pre-trip inspection and a driving test. Some states also require behind-the-wheel training, which is not included here.

Accepted in every state except Washington. Age and background-check requirements vary by state, so check with your state licensing agency.

FAQ

Is ELDT required before the Passenger (P) knowledge test?

Yes. Since February 2022, FMCSA has required entry-level drivers to complete approved training before taking the knowledge test for the P endorsement.

Is this the only training I need for the P endorsement?

No. You also need a medical exam from an examiner on FMCSA's National Registry of Certified Medical Examiners, the state passenger knowledge test, and a skills test that includes a pre-trip inspection and a driving test. A background check may also be required; check with your state licensing agency.

Is behind-the-wheel training included?

No. This course covers the ELDT theory requirement. If your state requires behind-the-wheel training, complete it separately with a provider listed on the FMCSA Training Provider Registry.

Is this course accepted in every state?

It is accepted in every state except Washington, which requires classroom instruction by a registered Washington training provider for drivers who hold or are obtaining a Washington CDL.
