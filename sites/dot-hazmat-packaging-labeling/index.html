FAQ

How is this different from the 4-hour general awareness course?

It adds function-specific lessons on specific packaging requirements and specific labeling requirements, for people who prepare, package and label hazmat shipments. It runs 5 hours and 12 lessons.

Which regulations does it cover?

The DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations in 49 CFR Parts 171-180, enforced by PHMSA. Hazmat training requirements are in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H.

How long is the certificate valid?

Three years. Hazmat employees must receive recurrent training at least once every three years; retake the course within 36 months to stay current.

Are there prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites, and the course is available in English and Spanish.
