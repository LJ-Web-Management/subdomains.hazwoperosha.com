FAQ

Who is this course for?

Supervisors and managers who oversee people preparing, handling or transporting hazmat by highway, plus compliance and safety officers, fleet and logistics managers, supervisory shipping, receiving and dispatch staff, and emergency response coordinators.

Do drivers need this course?

Drivers who handle, load or transport hazmat need DOT hazmat employee training, but this course is designed for supervisors. First-time drivers can take the CDL ELDT Hazmat (H) Endorsement course; drivers needing refresher training can take the 10-Hour DOT Hazmat Advanced General Awareness course.

Are there prerequisites?

No specific prerequisites, though a basic understanding of hazmat operations or prior hazmat employee training helps. It suits both new and experienced supervisors.

How long is the certificate valid?

Three years. Retake the course within 36 months to stay current with DOT recurrent training requirements.
