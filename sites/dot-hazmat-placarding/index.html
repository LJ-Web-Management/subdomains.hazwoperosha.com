FAQ

Which regulation does the course cover?

The placarding requirements in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart F of the DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations. DOT hazmat training requirements are in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H.

What's the difference between a label and a placard?

Labels go on individual packages. Placards are the larger signs displayed on transport vehicles, freight containers, rail cars and bulk packagings. The course explains the differences and when each applies.

How often must I retrain?

Hazmat employees must receive recurrent training at least once every three years, and training must be updated when DOT issues new or revised regulations that affect their job. The certificate is valid for three years.

Which transport modes does it address?

It explains how placarding applies across highway, rail, vessel and air, with special provisions for highway and rail.
