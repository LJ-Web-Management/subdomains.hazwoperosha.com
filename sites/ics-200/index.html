Questions & answers

What does ICS 200 cover that ICS 100 does not?

ICS 200 prepares supervisors for expanding incidents: activating Command and General Staff positions, transfer of command, unified command and Area Command structures, delegation of authority, management by objectives, and resource management and incident typing for Type 3 and Type 4 responses.

Is ICS 100 a prerequisite for ICS 200?

Yes. ICS 200 builds on ICS 100, Introduction to the Incident Command System, and completing ICS 100 is a prerequisite for enrollment.

Which standard does this ICS 200 course follow?

It is aligned with FEMA IS-200, Basic Incident Command System for Initial Response, and the National Incident Management System (NIMS).

How often should ICS 200 training be renewed?

FEMA does not set a fixed renewal frequency. Industry best practice is to retrain at least once every three years, and this course's certificate is valid for three years.
