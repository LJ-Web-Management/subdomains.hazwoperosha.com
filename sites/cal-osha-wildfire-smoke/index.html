When the sky turns orange, your crew needs a plan.

FAQ

Is wildfire smoke training required by Cal/OSHA?

Yes. Title 8, Section 5141.1 requires employers to train employees on wildfire smoke hazards, protective measures and emergency procedures. This course helps meet that requirement under Appendix B of Section 5141.1.

When must wildfire smoke training be renewed?

Cal/OSHA does not assign an expiration date. Retrain workers when duties, locations or smoke-exposure risks change, or when new controls or respirator use begins. An annual refresher each spring, around April or May, is strongly recommended ahead of California's peak wildfire season. The course certificate is valid for 12 months.

Does the course cover respirator requirements?

Yes. It explains when respirators are voluntary versus required under the wildfire smoke regulation. If respirator use becomes mandatory (typically when AQI exceeds 500), additional fit-testing and medical evaluation are required under Title 8, Section 5144, Respiratory Protection.

How long does the course take?

About 1 hour and 10 minutes across 9 lessons, followed by a final exam. The course is offered in English.
