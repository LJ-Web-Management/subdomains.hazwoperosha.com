FAQ

How often must employees receive workplace violence training in California?

Employers must train employees when the Workplace Violence Prevention Plan is established, then once every year, and again when the plan changes or a new hazard is identified.

What records must employers keep?

Violent incident logs, hazard records and investigation records must be kept for five years. Training records must be kept for one year.

Can employees report concerns without penalty?

Yes. California law protects employees who report threats or incidents, and the course explains how to report without fear of reprisal.

What is a Workplace Violence Prevention Plan (WVPP)?

A written plan explaining how an employer identifies, prevents and responds to workplace violence. It can stand alone or be part of the Injury and Illness Prevention Program (IIPP).

Which regulations does this course support?

California Labor Code Section 6401.9 (SB 553) and Title 8 CCR Section 3220 (Emergency Action Plan), supplemented by U.S. Department of Homeland Security and CISA active shooter preparedness guidance.
