FAQ

Which regulation does the course cover?

The marking requirements in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart D of the DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations. DOT hazmat training requirements are in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H.

What's the difference between marking and labeling?

Markings are the required text, numbers and marks on a package, such as the identification number. Labels are the diamond-shaped hazard class labels covered in 49 CFR 172 Subpart E and in our separate labeling course.

How often must I retrain?

The certificate is valid for three years. Retake the course before it expires to meet DOT recurrent training requirements.

Are there prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites, and the course is available in English and Spanish.
