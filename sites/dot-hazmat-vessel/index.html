FAQ

Which regulations does it cover?

The modal-specific requirements in 49 CFR Part 176 (Carriage by Vessel) of the DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations, and how the IMDG Code Dangerous Goods List compares with the HMR Hazardous Materials Table. DOT hazmat training requirements are in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H.

When must hazmat employees be trained?

DOT requires training within 90 days after employment or a change in job function, and recurrent training at least once every three years. The certificate is valid for three years.

Are there prerequisites?

No. It builds on the 4-hour DOT Hazmat Transportation General Awareness course, but there are no prerequisites. The course is available in English and Spanish.

Who should take it?

Shippers and carriers moving hazmat by vessel; dock, vessel and intermediary carrier personnel; ship's officers and crew who handle hazmat cargo; port and cargo security staff; anyone who packages, marks, labels, placards, loads or unloads hazmat for vessel shipment; and compliance officers.
