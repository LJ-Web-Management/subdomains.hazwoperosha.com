The 4 lessons, start to finish

  1. Lesson 1 · Overview

    Mobile Elevated Work Platforms overview

    MEWP types and components, when a lift is the right tool (and when it isn't), common hazards, and the key OSHA provisions.

  2. Lesson 2 · Before use

    Safety procedures before use

    Choosing the right MEWP, pre-operation inspection, verifying operator training, job hazard analysis and site risk assessment.

  3. Lesson 3 · During use

    Safety precautions during use

    Fall protection and tie-off, guardrails, stability and load limits, catapult hazards, power-line clearance and struck-by awareness.

  4. Lesson 4 · After use

    Safety procedures after use

    Securing, shutting down and parking the lift, post-use inspection, removing keys and storing equipment safely.

Followed by a final exam. The course gives awareness-level knowledge of lift safety, inspection, stability and hazard control.

FAQ

Which OSHA standards does the aerial and scissor lift course cover?

It meets OSHA's General Industry standard 29 CFR 1910.67 and the Construction Industry standards 29 CFR 1926.451 and 29 CFR 1926.453.

How long is the aerial lift certificate valid?

The certificate of completion is valid for three years (36 months). It is recommended that employers provide refresher training once every three years, so retake the course to renew.

How long does the course take?

About 2 hours. There are 4 lessons, taken in order, followed by a final exam. You have 3 months to finish and 1 year of access to the materials.

Are there any prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites for the OSHA Aerial – Scissor and Boom Lift Safety Training course.

When do I receive my certificate?

Immediately after passing the final exam you can download, save or print your certificate as a PDF.
