01 Who needs this training

OSHA requires TSDF employees exposed to hazardous waste to complete 24 hours of initial training under 29 CFR 1910.120(p)(7)(i) (general industry) and 29 CFR 1926.65(p)(7)(i) (construction).

  • Hazardous waste workers at treatment, storage and disposal facilities (TSDFs)
  • Employees handling, storing or disposing of RCRA hazardous waste
  • EHS professionals who manage compliance programs
  • Workers involved in waste treatment or disposal operations
  • Site supervisors overseeing hazardous waste facility operations

02 Syllabus: 64 lessons, 11 modules

Lessons are taken in order and end with a final examination.

  1. Regulatory Overview

    Federal laws, navigating the CFR, OSHA and the HAZWOPER standard, hazard communication, safety and health programs.

  2. Medical Surveillance & Toxicology

    Medical surveillance programs, toxicology basics, target organ toxicity and the factors that influence exposure.

  3. Hazard Classification

    Job hazard analysis, fire and oxygen deficiency, corrosives, solvents, oxidizers, reactives, explosives, radiological and biological hazards.

  4. Respiratory Protection

    Respirator types, selection factors, fit testing, training and maintenance.

  5. Personal Protective Equipment

    Chemical protective clothing, head-to-foot PPE, selection, donning and doffing, inspection and maintenance.

  6. HAZWOPER Site Operations

    Site characterization, site control, air monitoring, direct-reading instruments and personal sampling.

  7. Material Sampling & Analysis

    Field sampling plans, drum handling, opening, sampling, characterization and transport.

  8. Confined Spaces & Excavations

    Permit-required spaces, entry plans, rescue, soil classification and protective systems.

  9. Decontamination & Site Emergencies

    Decon facility design and methods, emergency decontamination and emergency documentation.

  10. EPA Cradle-to-Grave Program

    RCRA, hazardous waste generators, storage, transportation and TSDF responsibilities.

  11. Regulations for Specific Wastes

    Spills and emergencies, EPCRA and CERCLA, land disposal restrictions, recycling, used oil and universal waste.

05 Frequently asked questions

What are TSDF operations?

TSDF operations are the work performed at hazardous waste Treatment, Storage and Disposal Facilities, including collecting, sorting, treating (for example by incineration or oxidation) and disposing of hazardous waste.

Which OSHA standards does this 24-hour TSD course meet?

The course meets OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120(p)(7)(i) for general industry and 29 CFR 1926.65(p)(7)(i) for construction, under the HAZWOPER standard.

Are there any prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites for the OSHA 24-Hour HAZWOPER – RCRA TSD Operations Training course.

How long is the certificate valid?

The certificate of completion is valid for one year. You can download it as a PDF immediately after passing the final exam. Renew it each year with the OSHA 8-Hour HAZWOPER Annual Refresher – RCRA TSD Operations Training.

Do I get a wallet ID card?

Yes. Upload a passport-style photo to your HAZWOPER OSHA Training account and a plastic wallet card is mailed by U.S. mail within 2–3 weeks of course completion.

How long do I have to finish the course?

You have 3 months to complete the 24-hour course and 1 year of access to the training materials. The course is 100% online and available 24/7.
