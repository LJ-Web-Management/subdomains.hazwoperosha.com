FAQ

When is DOT hazmat training required?

Federal law requires initial training within 90 days of employment or a change in job function, and recurrent training at least every three years.

What does the course cover?

General awareness, function-specific instruction, modal requirements for highway, air, rail and vessel, and security awareness, under the Hazardous Materials Regulations in 49 CFR Parts 171-180.

How do I renew?

Take the same course again. The certificate is valid for three years.

Are there prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites, and the course is available in English and Spanish.
