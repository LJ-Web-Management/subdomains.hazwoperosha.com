FAQ

What is the CDL X endorsement?

The X endorsement combines the Hazardous Materials (H) and Tank Vehicle (N) endorsements, allowing a driver to transport hazardous liquids and gases in bulk tanks.

What else is required besides this training?

Drivers must pass the H and N knowledge tests and complete a Transportation Security Administration (TSA) background check, which includes identity verification, fingerprinting and a security threat assessment, and meet all federal and state requirements such as age, medical certification and driving eligibility.

Is this course accepted in every state?

It is accepted in every state except Washington, which requires classroom instruction by a registered Washington training provider for drivers who hold or are obtaining a Washington CDL.

How does the FMCSA know I completed the training?

Your records are sent to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry once you pass the final exam with 80% or more.
