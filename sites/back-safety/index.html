Questions

Why is back safety training important?

It reduces workplace injuries by teaching proper lifting, ergonomics and body mechanics, cuts absenteeism (back injuries are a leading cause of missed workdays), supports compliance with the OSHA General Duty Clause, and improves overall worker wellbeing.

Who should take back safety training?

Anyone who does physical work or spends long periods sitting or standing, especially workers in manufacturing, construction, healthcare, warehousing and transportation, as well as office workers.

How long is the course and certificate?

The course takes about 1 hour (2 lessons and a final exam). The certificate is valid for three years, and refresher training is recommended once every three years.

Are there any prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites for the Back Safety and Injury Prevention Training course.
