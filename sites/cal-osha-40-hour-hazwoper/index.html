FAQ

Does the online Cal/OSHA 40-hour course cover all HAZWOPER training?

It covers the education portion of 40-hour HAZWOPER training. Students must also complete three days of in-person field training led by a trained and experienced supervisor; employers provide that field training, including hands-on PPE experience and site-specific safety risks.

Who needs 40-hour HAZWOPER training in California?

Most workers new to uncontrolled hazardous waste or emergency response operations, including equipment operators, general laborers, other site workers, and on-site managers and supervisors, must complete 40 hours of HAZWOPER training under state and federal OSHA requirements.

What work do HAZWOPER standards cover?

Cleanup at hazardous waste sites, hazardous waste operations at treatment, storage and disposal (TSD) facilities, and emergency response operations involving hazardous substance releases.

How long is the certificate valid?

12 months. Keep it current each year with the Cal/OSHA 8-Hour HAZWOPER Refresher.

Are there any prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites for the Cal/OSHA 40-Hour HAZWOPER Training course.
