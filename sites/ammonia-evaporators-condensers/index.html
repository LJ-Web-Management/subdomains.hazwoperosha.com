Four lessons, four components

Each lesson focuses on one part of the heat-transfer and control side of an ammonia refrigeration system.

Lesson 1

Evaporators

Principles and types, flooded vs. dry expansion, controlled pressure receivers, heat-transfer factors, and uses in chillers, freezers, ice makers and intercoolers.

Lesson 2

Defrost systems

Why ice builds on coils, hot gas and soft hot gas defrost, and electric, water, glycol spray and room air methods.

Lesson 3

Evaporative condensers

Air-cooled vs. water-cooled vs. evaporative, components, selection, wet-bulb effects and Legionella control.

Lesson 4

Control valves & switches

Safety relief, hand expansion and float valves, shutoff valves, liquid level monitoring, and ASHRAE and ASME relief valve requirements.

FAQ

Who is this course for?

Professionals who operate, maintain or oversee industrial ammonia refrigeration systems, including refrigeration and HVAC technicians, chemical, process and environmental engineers, facilities managers, maintenance supervisors, industrial safety specialists and compliance officers.

Are there prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites, and the course is available in English and Spanish.

How long is the certificate valid?

You can download your certificate as soon as you pass. It is valid for three years, and employers are advised to provide refresher training every three years.

Does it cover safety relief valves?

Yes. Lesson 4 covers control valves and switches, including the ASHRAE and ASME standards and requirements for installing and setting safety relief valve pressure in ammonia systems.
