Know all nine hazard classes

Every hazmat shipment falls into one of the nine DOT hazard classes in 49 CFR Part 173. The diamonds below are simplified color cues, not actual placards.

  • 1Explosives
  • 2Gases
  • 3Flammable liquids
  • 4Flammable solids
  • 5Oxidizers & organic peroxides
  • 6Toxic & infectious substances
  • 7Radioactive
  • 8Corrosives
  • 9Miscellaneous

FAQ

Is ELDT required before the Hazmat (H) knowledge test?

Yes. Since February 2022, FMCSA has required entry-level drivers to complete approved training before taking the knowledge test for the H endorsement.

Is a TSA background check required?

Yes. The H endorsement requires a TSA security threat assessment, and renewing the endorsement requires the check again. Some states require more frequent assessments based on shorter license cycles.

Is this course accepted in every state?

It is accepted in every state except Washington, which requires classroom instruction by a registered Washington training provider for drivers who hold or are obtaining a Washington CDL.

What score do I need to pass?

80% or more on the final exam. Your completion is then reported to the FMCSA Training Provider Registry.
