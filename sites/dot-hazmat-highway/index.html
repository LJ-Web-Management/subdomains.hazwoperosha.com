FAQ

Does this course count as CDL ELDT for the Hazmat (H) endorsement?

No. This course does not fulfill the FMCSA Entry-Level Driver Training requirement for the Hazmat (H) endorsement. Drivers seeking the H endorsement need the separate CDL ELDT Hazmat (H) course.

Which regulations does it cover?

The modal-specific requirements in 49 CFR Part 177 (Carriage by Highway) of the Hazardous Materials Regulations and 49 CFR Parts 300-399 of the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations. DOT hazmat training requirements are in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H.

How often must I retrain?

Hazmat employees must receive recurrent training at least once every three years. The certificate is valid for three years.

Are there prerequisites?

No. It builds on the 4-hour DOT Hazmat Transportation General Awareness course, but there are no prerequisites. The course is available in English and Spanish.
