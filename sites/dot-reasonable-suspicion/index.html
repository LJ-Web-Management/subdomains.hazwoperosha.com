Alcohol thresholds

0.04 or greater breath alcohol concentration is a violation. 0.02 to 0.039 means the driver must be removed from safety-sensitive functions for at least 24 hours. Lesson 2 covers BAC levels and the actions employers must take.

FAQ

What is DOT reasonable suspicion training?

Training that helps designated supervisors recognize signs of alcohol misuse and controlled substance use, and understand when and how reasonable suspicion testing may be required under DOT regulations.

What is the 60/60 requirement?

FMCSA requires supervisors who make reasonable suspicion determinations to receive at least 60 minutes of training on alcohol misuse and at least 60 minutes on controlled substance use. Lesson 1 covers this requirement.

How often should supervisors retrain?

DOT does not set a refresher frequency for this training. Industry best practice is to refresh every two years.

Are there prerequisites?

No. There are no prerequisites, and the course is available in English and Spanish.
