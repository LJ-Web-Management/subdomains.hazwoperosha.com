Three things every security plan covers

The course describes the minimum considerations a hazmat security plan must address.

01

Personnel security

Confirming information provided by job applicants hired for positions with access to the hazmat covered by the plan.

02

Unauthorized access

Measures to keep unauthorized people away from hazmat and the transport conveyances being prepared.

03

En route security

Protecting hazmat from its origin to its destination, including while it is in storage incidental to movement.

FAQ

Which regulation does the course cover?

The security requirements in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart I of the DOT Hazardous Materials Regulations. DOT hazmat training requirements are in 49 CFR Part 172 Subpart H.

What must a security plan include?

At a minimum, a hazmat security plan addresses personnel security, unauthorized access, and en route security. The course describes each and how to build an effective plan.

How often must I retrain?

Hazmat employees must receive recurrent training at least once every three years, and training must be updated when DOT issues new or revised regulations that affect their job. The certificate is valid for three years.

Are there prerequisites?

No. It builds on the 4-hour DOT Hazmat Transportation General Awareness course, but there are no prerequisites. The course is available in English and Spanish.
