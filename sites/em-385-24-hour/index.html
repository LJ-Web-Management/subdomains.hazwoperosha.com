FAQ

Who is this course for?

Collateral Duty Safety Officers (CDSOs), supervisors, managers and team leads who oversee USACE or other federal government-contracted construction projects that follow EM 385-1-1.

Does it satisfy the CDSO refresher?

Yes. It fulfills the 24 hours of refresher training that CDSOs must complete every three years.

Is this course government-approved?

No. The federal government and its agencies do not approve or endorse any EM 385-1-1 training course. You receive a certificate of completion from HAZWOPER OSHA Training.

How long is the certificate valid?

Three years. There are no prerequisites, and the course is available in English and Spanish.
