Questions

How often do TSDF workers need HAZWOPER refresher training?

Every 12 months. OSHA's HAZWOPER standard requires annual refresher training, so TSDF workers complete this 8-hour RCRA TSD refresher once a year to keep their certification valid.

Are there prerequisites for the 8-hour TSD refresher?

Yes. You must already have completed initial HAZWOPER training, either the 24-hour or the 40-hour course depending on your job role. The refresher maintains an existing certification; it does not replace initial training. Need the initial course? See the 24-hour HAZWOPER RCRA TSD Operations training.

Which OSHA standards does the refresher meet?

It aligns with OSHA 29 CFR 1910.120(p) for general industry and 29 CFR 1926.65(p) for construction, the HAZWOPER provisions covering RCRA treatment, storage and disposal facilities.

When do I get my certificate and how long is it valid?

You can download, save or print your PDF certificate immediately after passing the final exam. It is valid for one year; retake the refresher annually to stay compliant.

Do I get a wallet ID card?

Yes. Upload a passport-style photo to your HAZWOPER OSHA Training account and a plastic wallet card is mailed by U.S. mail within 2–3 weeks of course completion.
