The OSHA angle

There's no specific OSHA standard on substance abuse. The General Duty Clause, Section 5(a)(1) of the OSH Act, requires employers to provide a workplace free from recognized hazards, and awareness training is one way to address impairment risk.

FAQ

Is there an OSHA standard for drug and alcohol use?

There is no specific OSHA standard on substance abuse. The General Duty Clause, Section 5(a)(1) of the OSH Act, requires employers to provide a workplace free from recognized hazards, and awareness training helps address impairment risks.

Who should take this training?

Construction workers who perform safety-sensitive tasks on active jobsites, where alertness, coordination and judgment are critical. It is also relevant to large projects, public works and federal contracts that may require a formal Drug-Free Workplace Program.
